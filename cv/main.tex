I aim to develop agents that make reliable decisions when training environments and reward signals imperfectly reflect real-world conditions and user goals. I am particularly interested in aligning training environments with intended tasks and learning policies that remain robust to uncertainty and changes in the environment. \\

My research in reinforcement learning and generative modeling provides a foundation for this goal through efficient exploration and reliable decision-making. I develop off-policy reinforcement learning methods to generate high-quality and diverse candidates, and generative model-based methods for optimization in complex search spaces. My work spans LLM post-training, text-to-image generation, biological sequence design, and simulation-based optimization, including traffic control and infrastructure layout design.
